Privacy policy

hwp MCP is an MCP server for HWP and HWPX documents, operated by Young Joon Lee. Contact: yj.lee@chu.ac.kr.

This page describes exactly what the service stores. Where it says something is not stored, that is enforced in code, not policy.

What Google gives us

Signing in requests only the openid, email and profile scopes. From the resulting token the service reads three claims and nothing else: your Google account identifier (sub), your email address, and your display name. They identify your account. They are never sold, never shared, never used for advertising, and never used to train models. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

What the service stores

Your account. An internal identifier, the Google sub, your email address, your display name, and the times you first signed in and last signed in. Identity is keyed on sub rather than on the email address, because an address can be reassigned to a different person.

Access tokens you create. An identifier, a label you chose, a SHA-256 hash of the token, and the times it was created, last used and revoked. The token itself is shown once at creation and is never stored.

An activity log, metadata only. A timestamp, a request identifier, your account identifier, a SHA-256 hash of the session identifier, the name of the tool or method called, whether it succeeded, how long it took, and how many bytes moved in and out. It contains no tool arguments, no file names, no paths, no document content and no tokens.

Sign-in cookies. A signed dashboard session cookie lasting twelve hours, a cookie preventing cross-site request forgery, and a signed cookie recording which client you already approved, lasting ninety days. All are signed rather than tracking, and there are no third-party cookies and no analytics.

Your documents

Documents you upload or generate exist only inside a container created for your session. That container has no network access to anywhere, is private to your session, and is destroyed when the session ends. Document content is never written to durable storage, never logged, and never read by the operator.

Who else is involved

Cloudflare provides the infrastructure the service runs on, and Google provides sign-in. No one else receives your data.

Retention and deletion

Your account record and your tokens are deleted on request to yj.lee@chu.ac.kr; there is no self-service deletion page today. The metadata-only activity log described above is currently retained without a fixed expiry. Documents need no deletion request because they do not outlive the session that created them.
